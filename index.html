Privacy by design

The safe choice is the default choice.

Masked by default

Your Aadhaar is shared in UIDAI's masked format (last 4 digits) unless you choose otherwise. Date of birth and address start hidden.

Links that expire

Every share has an expiry and a purpose label. When it's over, it stops opening, for everyone.

Take it back

One place shows every link and every app with access, with views and time left. Revoke with one tap, and undo if you slipped.

Proof you can see

Documents sent by an issuer carry a fine-line seal. Files you uploaded yourself never do, so you always know which copy counts.

Locks itself

PIN or fingerprint to open, and it re-locks after five minutes away, which matters on a shared phone.

English or हिन्दी

Every screen in both languages, written the way people speak, not translated word for word.

Questions

Is this the official DigiLocker app?+

No. DigiLocker, Unlocked is a student concept redesign made for a university course. It isn't affiliated with MeitY, NeGD or DigiLocker, and the prototype uses only made-up documents.

Will you ask for my Aadhaar, DigiLocker login or OTP?+

Never. The sign-up below asks only for your name, email, college and year. If anyone claiming to be us asks for an ID number or OTP, it isn't us.

What do I get for signing up?+

The first 500 sign-ups get early access to the beta prototype and a Founding Tester badge. You may be invited to a 15-minute test session (always optional).

What happens to my email?+

We use it only to invite you to the beta and testing, and delete the list when the project ends. No marketing, no sharing.

Can I try it right now?+

Yes: the interactive prototype runs in your browser and installs on your phone. Everything in it is fictional.
